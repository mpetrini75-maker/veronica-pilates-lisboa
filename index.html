THE ARTIST BEHIND THE METHOD

From the German Stage
to Lisbon's Most Precise Reformer Practice.

Born in Milan and shaped by the world-renowned Hamburg Ballet Academy, Veronica spent years performing as a professional dancer in Berlin's elite contemporary and theatre productions (including the prestigious Friedrichstadt-Palast).

After discovering how Pilates revolutionized her own physical longevity and injury resilience, she completed the rigorous STOTT Pilates® certification. Today, as a lead trainer in Lisbon, she bridges the grace of classical stage artistry with contemporary sports science.
